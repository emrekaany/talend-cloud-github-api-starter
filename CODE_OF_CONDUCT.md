# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a technically rigorous, welcoming community where people can ask questions and challenge ideas without being diminished. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with respect and assume neither expertise nor intent;
- critique code, evidence, and decisions rather than people;
- make space for different backgrounds, languages, abilities, and experience levels;
- protect credentials, client confidentiality, and personal information;
- correct mistakes transparently and accept proportionate feedback;
- avoid marketing claims that are not supported by reviewable evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private or confidential information are not acceptable. Neither is pressuring someone to share a token, private project, client file, live identifier, or security detail in a public channel.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in a public forum.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct concerns by exposing private details in a public issue. Use GitHub's platform reporting tools when the behavior occurs on GitHub. If a project-maintainer route is needed, ask for one in a [non-sensitive Discussion](https://github.com/emrekaany/talend-cloud-github-api-starter/discussions) without naming people or describing private details.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct process. Never include credentials, client artifacts, or live environment details in either route.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behavior violates this standard. Decisions should consider severity, impact, context, and repeated behavior. Maintainers should protect reporter privacy as far as practical and disclose conflicts of interest.

This policy was written for this repository and is not a guarantee of response time, confidentiality under every legal circumstance, or professional mediation.
